# Google Ads Case Study: B2B Lead Generation 🏢

## 📌 The Concept
A simulated Google Search campaign for "Gulf Leadership Institute," a hypothetical corporate training company in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. 

*   **The Goal:** Generate high-quality leads (HR Directors & CEOs) for a $3,000 Executive Masterclass.
*   **The Budget:** $1,500/month.
*   **The Challenge:** B2B clicks are expensive. We cannot afford to waste budget on students or job seekers.

## 🎯 Specialist Targeting Strategy
Because this is a high-ticket B2B offer, we use **Exact Match** to capture people actively looking for solutions.
*   **Top Keywords:** "executive leadership course riyadh", "corporate training for managers"
*   **Crucial Negative Keywords:** -free, -jobs, -salary, -students (This ensures we only pay for clicks from actual decision-makers).

## ✍️ Creative Strategy
In B2B, you don't ask for the sale immediately. The ad copy focuses on authority and offers a **"Brochure Download"** rather than a "Buy Now" button. The campaign is fully bilingual to accommodate Saudi executives and expats.

## 📊 Forecasting & ROI
*   **Estimated CPC:** $5.00 (High due to B2B competition)
*   **Clicks:** 300
*   **Conversion Rate:** 5% (Downloading a brochure is an easier ask than buying)
*   **Projected Leads:** 15
*   **Cost Per Lead (CPL):** $100
*(If one lead converts into a $3,000 sale, the ROI on this $1,500 budget is massive).*

## ⚙️ Tracking
I would implement Google Tag Manager to track the PDF download as a conversion, and use dynamic call tracking to see which keywords generate the most phone calls.
